## Recommended tools

### Exhibitor Intelligence

- [Lensmor](https://www.lensmor.com/) — AI-powered exhibitor intelligence, event discovery, and pre-show lead insights

### CRM

- [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/) — Enterprise CRM with event attribution and pipeline tracking
- [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/) — Mid-market CRM with event tracking and sequences
- [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com/) — Sales-focused CRM with activity tracking

### Lead Capture

- [Cvent LeadCapture](https://www.cvent.com/) — Badge scanning and lead capture for events
- [iCapture](https://www.icapture.com/) — Trade show lead capture with offline sync
- [Akkroo](https://www.akkroo.com/) — GDPR-friendly lead capture

### Scheduling

- [Calendly](https://calendly.com/) — Meeting scheduling with calendar integration
- [Chili Piper](https://www.chilipiper.com/) — Revenue scheduling for sales teams

### Data Enrichment

- [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/) — B2B data and outreach sequences
- [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/) — Enterprise B2B intelligence
- [Clay](https://www.clay.com/) — Data enrichment and outreach automation

### Email Outreach

- [Outreach](https://www.outreach.io/) — Sales engagement platform with sequencing
- [Salesloft](https://www.salesloft.com/) — Sales engagement and cadence management
- [Instantly](https://www.instantly.ai/) — Cold email and outreach at scale
